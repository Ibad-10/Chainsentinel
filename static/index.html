Overview

Is this crypto wallet laundering money? ChainSentinel pulls its public transactions, eight rules gather evidence, and Banyan Sapling judges that evidence against a written anti-money-laundering policy.

  1. Paste a walletAny public Ethereum or Bitcoin address, or pick an example below.
  2. See the money flowA map of where the money came from and went, with suspicious flows highlighted.
  3. Get a verdict with reasonsSapling scores the evidence against the policy: FLAG, REVIEW or CLEAR.

Pattern lab

Synthetic  Laundering shapes from the IBM AMLworld typologies plus crypto-specific ones, generated with random addresses by backend/synth.py. Each is drawn, run through the rules and sent to Sapling. The highlighted flows are what the rules matched.

Watchlist
